The Short Version
  • This is a well-built site and nobody should rebuild it. Nineteen separate service pages, correct structured data, HTTPS, mobile-formatted, three tappable numbers on every page. The expensive part is done.
  • Google Tag Manager is installed and empty. Container GTM-548KL6C6 loads on every page with no GA4 behind it. No analytics, no conversion tracking, no call tracking, no ads tags. Nobody can tell you where a single one of your 3–4 weekly jobs came from.
  • Nineteen pages and not one town. "Fife" appears 11 times. Glenrothes, Kirkcaldy, Dunfermline, St Andrews, Leven, Cupar, Methil, Lochgelly, Cowdenbeath and Burntisland appear zero times between them — and people search by town, not by county.
  • You prefer the shorter runs. Town pages are how you bias enquiries toward them instead of taking whatever the county sends.
  • Your enquiry form is on 1 page out of 19. It is a real, working Gravity Form on /contact-us/ collecting name, phone, email and postcode — and it is the only one. Every service page has phone numbers and no form.
  • Checkatrade brings you the work and your site mentions it once. No rating, no review count, no review text, no job count anywhere on the homepage. You compete on price on their platform and show no proof at all on your own.
  • £100 and £20,000 arrive through the same door. Roof cleaning and guttering sit level with new roofs, with no qualifying question anywhere. At 3–4 jobs a week the question is not how many enquiries, it is which ones get your Tuesday.
  • Content is thin where it counts. Homepage 506 words, roof repairs 522, contact 271.
  • Credit where it is due: Organization, LocalBusiness, PostalAddress, WebSite and BreadcrumbList schema are all present and correct. Most roofers we audit have none of it.
  • The first job is an afternoon's work: put GA4 into the container that is already sitting there, and track form submits and phone taps.
